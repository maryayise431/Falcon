The Process

From vendor shelf
to customer door.

01
VENDOR SENDS STOCK
A registered vendor lists products and ships inventory to a Falcon warehouse, where we store and manage it for them.
02
CUSTOMER ORDERS
Vendors get an order and inputs customer details.
03
AGENT ASSIGNED
The nearest available delivery agent is automatically matched to the order and picks it up from the warehouse.
04
TRACKED & DELIVERED
The customer tracks the order live until it arrives, and the vendor's remittance is calculated automatically.
